Data sources

River levels: Jabatan Pengairan dan Saliran (JPS) Malaysia, read live on the machine running VEDA and used for non-commercial public-safety research. JPS readings are never redistributed.

  • JPS Infobanjir · river levels and their Normal / Alert / Warning / Danger marks
  • data.gov.my · MetMalaysia forecasts and warnings, and the flood-warning feed
  • Open-Meteo · rain, under CC BY 4.0
  • © OpenStreetMap contributors · rivers, coast, places, schools and halls, bridges - under the ODbL

Past flood replay (Nov 2024): readings as reported by Bernama, Harian Metro and Kosmo, citing JPS - each one cited with its link. Made-up test scenes use invented readings and say so.
