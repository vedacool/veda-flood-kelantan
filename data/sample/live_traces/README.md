is a `{"redaction": …}` record saying the same. The unredacted originals are kept privately by the team outside the
repo and are never committed. Every river level derived
from a JPS reading - the readings, their changes, the distances to Alert / Danger (they reveal the reading), the
projected ranges, the values inside the AI explanations and the reconcile values - reads
`[JPS reading — not redistributed]`. Kept as they were: every step, the timings, the Critic's results and reasons,
the cited JPS thresholds and historic peaks, Open-Meteo rain (mm), times and run ids.
